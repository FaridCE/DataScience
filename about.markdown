My name is **Alejandro Farid Cantun Estrella**. I am a **Mexican physicist** with interests in **cosmology, semiclassical gravity, mathematical physics, quantitative finance, and data science**.

My research background in theoretical cosmology has provided me with a strong foundation in statistical inference, linear algebra, spectral methods, and computational modeling. I enjoy applying these quantitative tools to multidisciplinary problems, particularly in financial data analysis, portfolio optimization, and risk modeling.

This website showcases a collection of projects exploring machine learning, statistical modeling, quantitative finance, and scientific computing, demonstrating how techniques developed in physics can be adapted to solve real-world data-driven problems.
